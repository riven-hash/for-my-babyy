Everything I haven't said out loud

I don't know if I say this enough, so I wanted to write it down where it can't get lost in a busy day or a rushed goodnight text: I love you. Not the easy, convenient kind of love, the kind that's still choosing you on the hard days, the quiet days, the days you pull away without meaning to.

I've seen your past come up in the middle of an ordinary afternoon and change the whole shape of it. I've seen you brace for the moment I leave, even when I'm not going anywhere. I'm not writing this to fix that or to rush you out of it. I'm writing it so you have something to come back to when that voice gets loud: I'm not here despite who you are. I'm here because of who you are, all of it, the guarded parts included.

You don't have to perform okay-ness for me. You don't have to explain the silence or apologize for needing space. I'd rather have the real, sometimes-distant you than a version of you that's just managing my expectations. Whatever it takes, however long it takes, I'm not in a hurry, and I'm not going anywhere.
